# Marketing & SEO Content Standards

## Purpose

This document defines the marketing content structure, SEO standards, and page generation strategy for the Paywall Link platform.

The goal is to:

- capture organic creator traffic
- rank for long-tail monetization queries
- be easily indexed by search engines
- be easily understood by AI assistants and agents
- scale content automatically as the platform grows

Primary traffic sources:

- Google search
- AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity)
- creator communities
- Reddit discussions
- social media links

---

# Core SEO Positioning

The product should consistently be described as:

> **The easiest way to monetize any link.**

Core product explanation:

Paste link → Set price → Share → Get paid

Secondary messaging:

- Sell digital resources instantly
- Paywall any URL
- Monetize templates, files, and datasets
- Sell digital content without a storefront

---

# Target SEO Topics

The platform focuses on creator monetization queries.

Primary topics:

- selling digital products

# Page Structure Standard

Every SEO page should follow the same structure.

## 1. Title

Clear and descriptive.

Example:

How to Sell Notion Templates | Platform Name

---

## 2. Intro Section

Explain the creator problem.

Example:

> Selling Notion templates online usually requires creating a storefront, uploading files, and managing payment systems. With Platform Name, you can monetize any Notion template link instantly.

---

## 3. Problem Explanation

Explain why the problem exists.

Example topics:

- marketplaces take large fees
- storefront setup is complicated
- creators want direct sales

---

## 4. Solution Section

Explain the platform workflow.

Example:

Paste your template link
Set a price
Share the paywall URL
Get paid instantly

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Guide

Clear instructions.

Example:

	1.	Create your template
	2.	Copy the share link
	3.	Paste it into the platform
	4.	Set your price
	5.	Share your unlock link

---

## 6. Creator Examples

Provide realistic use cases.

Example:

- Notion productivity systems
- SaaS dashboards
- client CRM templates

---

## 7. Call to Action

Encourage platform usage.

Example:

> Start selling your templates in minutes.

---

# AI Discoverability Standards

AI assistants increasingly generate answers from structured web content.

To optimize for AI discovery:

- use clear headings
- use direct answers to questions
- include short explanations
- avoid ambiguous marketing language

Example section:

Can you sell Notion templates online?

Yes. You can monetize a Notion template by creating a duplicate link and placing it behind a paywall.

AI tools extract this information easily.

---

# Content Formatting Rules

Content must be:

- scannable
- structured
- concise

Use:

headings
bullet lists
short paragraphs
code blocks

Avoid:

- long walls of text
- vague marketing statements

---

# Internal Linking Strategy

Pages should link to each other to strengthen SEO authority.

Example links:

sell-notion-template → paywall-link
sell-ai-prompts → sell-digital-downloads
monetize-google-drive → sell-digital-products

This improves crawlability.
