# unseal.link — Product Brief for Marketing

---

## One-Line Positioning

> **The easiest way to sell anything with a URL.**

---

## What It Does

unseal.link lets creators, freelancers, and developers turn any link into a paid product in under 60 seconds.

Paste a URL. Set a price. Share the paywall. Buyers pay once via Stripe and receive instant access by email — no account required, no storefront, no file uploads.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Creators lose money every day because selling digital products is unnecessarily complicated:

- Platforms like Gumroad take 10% of every sale
- Setting up a storefront takes days
- Freelancers deliver work before payment clears — and get ghosted
- Buyers abandon purchases that require account sign-ups

unseal.link removes every one of these friction points.

---

## Core Features

**For sellers**
- Paste any URL (Notion, Figma, Google Drive, GitHub, Discord, or any HTTPS link)
- Set a price from $9.99 — no minimum commitment, no monthly fees
- Connect Stripe once — all payouts land directly in your bank account
- Live preview of your paywall before publishing
- Optional: preview image, limited-time offer countdown, custom description
- Dashboard with revenue chart, sales history, and order management
- Full refund control from the dashboard
- One-click link archiving and reactivation

**For buyers**
- No account required — pay and receive access by email in under 30 seconds
- Secure single-use access link delivered by Stripe-verified email
- Order history accessible at any time by email

**Platform**
- 4.5% platform fee per sale — half the rate of Gumroad
- Stripe handles all payments — the platform never touches seller funds
- Abuse reporting built into every paywall page
- Public seller profile page at `/@username`

## Key Differentiators

1. **Zero storefront** — no product pages to design, no file uploads, no hosting. If it has a URL, you can sell it.
2. **No buyer friction** — buyers don't create accounts. Pay → email → done. This alone increases conversion.
3. **Payment-first by design** — the link only unlocks after Stripe confirms payment. Ghosting is structurally impossible.
4. **Direct to bank** — money goes straight into the seller's Stripe account. unseal.link never holds funds.
5. **Lowest fees in the category** — 4.5% beats every major alternative.
