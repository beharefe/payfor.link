Purpose

This document defines the initial monetization strategy for the Paywall Link platform during the MVP stage.

The goal is to:
	•	minimize onboarding friction for sellers
	•	validate product-market fit quickly
	•	generate early platform revenue
	•	keep pricing simple and transparent

The MVP revenue model must remain simple, predictable, and easy to understand.

⸻

Core Pricing Model

The MVP uses a single transaction-based fee.

Platform fee:

4.5% per successful transaction

This fee is applied to every purchase made through the platform.

Example:

Product Price	Platform Fee (4.5%)	Seller Receives
$10	$0.45	$9.55
$25	$1.13	$23.87
$100	$4.50	$95.50

Stripe payment processing fees apply separately.

⸻

Why 4.5%?

The selected fee positions the platform competitively in the creator commerce market.

Platform	Platform Fee
Gumroad	~10%
Lemon Squeezy	~5%
Paddle	~5%
Ko-fi shop	~5%
Our platform	4.5%

This makes the platform:
	•	cheaper than most competitors
	•	sustainable for early-stage revenue
	•	simple for sellers to understand

⸻

Platform Fee Implementation

Fees will be collected using Stripe Connect application fees.

Payment flow:

Buyer pays product price
↓
Stripe processes payment
↓
Platform fee (4.5%) deducted automatically
↓
Remaining amount sent to seller Stripe account

Stripe distributes funds automatically.

The platform never holds seller money directly.

⸻

Seller Cost Breakdown

Sellers pay two fees:

1. Platform Fee

4.5%

Collected by the platform.

⸻

2. Stripe Processing Fee

Stripe charges payment processing fees depending on region.

Typical example:

~2.9% + $0.30 per transaction

These fees go directly to Stripe.

⸻

Why No Subscription Plans at Launch

Subscription pricing is intentionally avoided during the MVP stage.

Reasons:
	•	new creators may not yet have revenue
	•	subscription friction reduces onboarding conversion
	•	usage patterns are unknown in early stages

The MVP prioritizes:

seller adoption
transaction volume
product validation


⸻

Future Pricing Strategy (Post-MVP)

After the platform achieves traction, optional subscription tiers may be introduced.

Example structure:

Plan	Monthly Price	Platform Fee
Free	$0	4.5%
Starter	$19	3%
Grow	$49	2%
Pro	$99	1%

Upgrade incentives will be based on seller transaction volume.

Example dashboard message:

You paid $72 in fees this month.
Upgrade to Grow and save $24/month.

Core Revenue Principle

The platform earns money only when sellers earn money.

Core philosophy:

Creator success = Platform success

This alignment builds trust and encourages adoption.

⸻

Pricing Communication

Pricing must be communicated clearly on marketing pages.

Recommended messaging:

Start selling for free.
Only pay 4.5% when you make a sale.

Avoid complicated pricing during MVP.

⸻

Key Metrics

During MVP stage track:

GMV (Gross Merchandise Volume)
Total transactions
Average product price
Active sellers
Platform fee revenue

Early traction targets:

20 sellers
100 purchases


⸻

Summary

The MVP pricing strategy focuses on simplicity and growth.

Pricing model:

4.5% platform fee per transaction

No subscriptions or tiered plans will be introduced until real usage data is collected.

The primary goal during MVP stage is seller adoption and transaction volume.
