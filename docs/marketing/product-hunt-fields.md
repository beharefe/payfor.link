## Maker First Comment

Posted immediately at launch. Copy-paste ready:

---

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I built unseal.link after watching a designer friend deliver a complete $2,400 Figma project — and never get paid. The client had the files. There was nothing left to threaten. Invoice ignored.

The fix seemed obvious: payment before delivery. But when I looked at what tools existed, every option required either a full storefront (Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy), a file upload, or 10% of every sale.

None of them solved the actual problem: you have something at a URL, and you need a payment gate in front of it right now.

**unseal.link is that payment gate.**

Paste any URL — Notion, Figma, Google Drive, Discord, GitHub, Canva, anything. Set a price. You get a paid access link. Buyers pay via Stripe and unlock instantly on the confirmation page. No buyer account needed. No storefront to build. 4.5% fee.

The use cases that surprised me during building:
- Freelancers paywalling final deliverables so ghosting is structurally impossible
- Educators selling a Google Drive course folder without paying $150/month for Kajabi
- Discord community builders selling invite links
- Figma designers still selling after Figma closed paid Community submissions in 2023

Would genuinely love your feedback — especially on the onboarding. What's unclear when you first land?
