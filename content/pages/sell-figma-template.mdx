## The Figma Community problem

Figma Community was a way to distribute templates and UI kits for free or at low prices. It was never designed for serious digital product monetization: limited pricing control, limited buyer data, no storefront customization, and Figma controls the whole relationship.

The more effective model: sell directly. You own the buyer relationship, you set the price, you keep 95.5% of every sale. Your file stays exactly where it is on Figma — you just add a payment gate in front of the share link.

## What Figma products sell well

### UI kits and component libraries
Full design systems, component libraries, or focused kits (dashboards, e-commerce, mobile apps). Price range: $29–$149. Value comes from the depth of coverage and how production-ready the components are.

### Icon sets and illustration packs
Custom icon families, illustration styles, or themed asset sets. Price range: $15–$79. Buyers want consistent style and broad coverage — aim for 100+ icons minimum.

### Templates for specific use cases
Landing page templates, mobile app UIs, SaaS dashboard layouts, presentation decks. Price range: $19–$89. The more specific the use case (e.g. "B2B SaaS pricing page kit"), the better it converts.

### Design systems and style guides
More complete than a UI kit — includes tokens, spacing, typography, color palettes, and documentation. Price range: $49–$299. These take longer to produce but justify higher prices.

### Wireframe and prototyping kits
Low-fidelity wireframe libraries for rapid prototyping. Price range: $19–$59. Popular with UX designers who need to move fast before high-fidelity work.

## Pricing your Figma template

A few principles that hold across Figma products:

**Anchor on hours saved, not effort spent.** A dashboard UI kit that saves a designer 20 hours is worth $49–$99 regardless of how long it took you to make.

**Start higher than you think.** Most first-time sellers underprice. A $29 template and a $79 template convert similarly — buyers are already spending time researching and evaluating. The price is rarely the deciding factor for buyers who like your style.

**Preview image is the real conversion lever.** Your paywall description helps, but buyers decide on the screenshot. Show your best work — the densest, most polished frame in your file.

## How this compares to the alternatives

### Gumroad
Gumroad works for Figma files but requires you to export and upload a file. If you update your design, you need to re-export and re-upload. Buyers download a static snapshot, not a live Figma link. Plus 10% fee vs. 4.5%.

### Figma Community
Limited pricing flexibility, Figma controls discovery, and you can't reach buyers outside the platform. Better for free distribution to grow an audience than for monetization.

### Direct invoice or DM
Works for one-off sales but doesn't scale. Every sale requires manual effort. No scalable paywall link you can share indefinitely.
