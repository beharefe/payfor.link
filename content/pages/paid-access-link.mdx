## What is a paid access link?

A paid access link is a URL that gates another URL behind a Stripe payment. You have content somewhere — a Notion template, a Figma file, a Google Drive folder, a Discord server, a GitHub repo — and you want to charge for access to it.

Instead of building a checkout flow or setting up a storefront, you create a paid access link: a public-facing URL that holds your content URL behind a payment form. Buyers visit the link, see your title and price, pay, and the destination unlocks instantly.

Your content stays exactly where it already lives. The paid access link is just the payment gate in front of it.

## Who uses paid access links

**Template creators** — Notion systems, Figma UI kits, Canva templates, Google Sheets models. The product lives at a URL. The paid access link is the payment gate.

**Freelancers** — Final deliverables go into a Google Drive folder. The folder link goes behind a paid access link. Client pays, client gets the files. No invoice chasing, no delivery before payment.

**Educators** — Course videos in a Google Drive folder, curriculum in Notion, recorded workshops on Loom. A single paid access link gates the whole thing without a course platform.

**Community builders** — Discord server invites, private newsletter access, gated Slack groups. A paid access link makes any invite URL a product.

**Developers** — Boilerplates and starter kits, private repos, deployment guides, license files. Link buyers directly to what they're buying.

## How this is different from other tools

**vs. Gumroad**: Gumroad requires file uploads and charges 10%. Paid access links work with any URL and charge 4.5%. If your product already lives at a URL, you're paying 5.5% extra for a file-upload workflow you don't need.

**vs. Stripe Payment Links**: Stripe Payment Links processes payments. It delivers nothing. You'd still manually email every buyer. A paid access link automates the entire delivery.

**vs. building a checkout**: Writing a Stripe integration from scratch takes days. Creating a paid access link takes 2 minutes. Same Stripe infrastructure, zero code.
