## The short version

- **Stan Store**: A "link in bio" creator store optimized for social media audiences. Courses, memberships, digital downloads, 1:1 bookings — all in one profile. Free plan charges 5% per sale; Pro plan at $29/mo removes the fee.
- **unseal.link**: No profile, no storefront, no course builder. Paste any URL behind a paywall. Buyers pay via Stripe and get the link by email in under 30 seconds. 4.5% flat, no monthly cost.

Stan Store is the right tool if you want a full creator business hub — multiple products, a public profile, and a "link in bio" destination. unseal.link is the right tool if you want to sell one thing fast without building a storefront.

## When Stan Store makes more sense

**High volume**: Stan Store Pro costs $29/mo and removes the per-sale fee. If you are doing 60+ sales of a $49 product monthly, the math tilts toward Stan Store Pro: $29/mo beats 4.5% × 60 × $49 = $132.30/mo.

**Multi-product storefront**: Stan Store is built for creators who want a single "link in bio" destination. Courses, digital downloads, 1:1 calls, memberships — all in one place with a public profile. If your audience follows you and expects to browse your catalog, Stan Store is designed for that.

**Courses and memberships**: Stan Store has a full course builder and membership tiers. If you want hosted video content and recurring subscriptions, Stan Store does that natively. unseal.link does not.

## When unseal.link makes more sense

**Low to medium volume**: Until you hit ~60 sales/month of a $49 product, the math favors unseal.link's 4.5% over Stan Store's $29/mo Pro plan. The free tier of Stan Store actually costs more per sale (5%) than unseal.link (4.5%).

**Link-based products**: Stan Store requires uploads or integrations. If you sell a Notion template, Figma file, or Google Drive resource, Stan Store forces you to upload the file or set up an integration. unseal.link treats any URL as a product directly.

**Fast setup**: Stan Store's full setup — profile, product listings, branding — takes 60–90 minutes. unseal.link takes under 5 minutes per product. For freelancers or one-off sales, that overhead is not worth it.

**No monthly commitment**: unseal.link has no monthly fee. You pay when you sell. For seasonal sellers or creators just getting started, not paying $29/mo during slow months matters.
