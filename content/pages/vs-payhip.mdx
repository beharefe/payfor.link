## The short version

- **Payhip**: A full digital product platform with storefronts, affiliate tools, EU VAT handling, and course hosting. Three tiers: Free (5% fee), Plus ($29/mo, 2% fee), Pro ($99/mo, 0% fee). File uploads required.
- **unseal.link**: No storefront, no course builder. Paste any URL, set a price, share the paywall. 4.5% flat, no monthly cost. No file uploads.

Payhip is the right tool if you want a feature-complete storefront with EU tax compliance and optional affiliate management. unseal.link is the right tool if you want the lowest total cost for link-based product delivery and do not need a storefront.

## The fee ladder that may not help you

Payhip's pricing is:
- **Free**: 5% per sale
- **Plus**: $29/mo + 2% per sale
- **Pro**: $99/mo + 0% per sale

The "0% fee" on Pro sounds great. But you need to sell enough to justify $99/mo:

At **$49 product**:
- Payhip Free: 5% = $2.45/sale
- unseal.link: 4.5% = $2.205/sale — cheaper immediately, no monthly fee
- Payhip Pro breaks even vs. unseal.link when fees on unseal.link exceed $99/mo → that requires ~2,200 sales of a $49 product monthly

For most independent creators, Payhip Pro's $99/mo means paying for 0% fees on volume you are not doing yet.

## When Payhip wins

**International VAT compliance**: Payhip is a Merchant of Record for EU VAT and UK VAT. If you sell to European buyers and want automatic tax compliance without thinking about it, Payhip handles it. This is a real advantage for creators selling globally at volume.

**Full storefront**: Payhip gives you a public profile with all your products, customer email collection, and branding. If buyers need to browse your catalog, Payhip supports that properly.

**Affiliate marketing**: Payhip's built-in affiliate program lets other creators promote your products for a commission. For products with broad appeal, this can compound distribution.

**Course hosting**: Plus and Pro plans include video course hosting. If you want hosted content delivery with structured lessons, Payhip does that. unseal.link does not.

## When unseal.link wins

**Lower total cost without a monthly fee**: Payhip's free plan charges 5% — more than unseal.link's 4.5%. To get below unseal.link's rate, you need to pay at least $29/mo. Until your volume justifies that, unseal.link is cheaper.

**Link-based products**: If your product is a Notion template, Figma file, Google Drive folder, or any URL-accessible resource, Payhip requires you to export it to a downloadable file. unseal.link takes the URL directly.

**No payout minimum**: Payhip has a $50 payout minimum. Your first sales are held until you clear $50. unseal.link has no payout minimum — first sale pays out immediately via your Stripe account.
