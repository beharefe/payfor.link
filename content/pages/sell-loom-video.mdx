## When a video is worth selling

Not every video needs a course platform. Some recordings are genuinely valuable as one-time purchases:

- **Code walkthroughs**: A 45-minute deep-dive into your architecture, a refactoring session, a debugging walkthrough
- **Design critiques**: A recorded UI/UX review of someone's product, annotated screen recording
- **Business system explanations**: How you run your operations, your sales process, your content workflow
- **Consulting recordings**: A one-time strategy session you recorded with permission
- **Tutorial packs**: A set of focused recordings on one topic (e.g. "5 videos on CSS grid")
- **Behind-the-scenes content**: How you built something, your creative process

Building a full Teachable or Podia course for a single video is overkill. Uploading to Gumroad is more friction than the product requires. The video is already hosted somewhere. It has a URL.

## How to get a shareable link from each platform

### Loom

1. Open your Loom recording
2. Click **Share** → set to **Anyone with the link**
3. Copy the link

That's it. The Loom URL is what you paste into unseal.link. Buyers who pay receive the Loom link by email and watch it directly.

### Vimeo

1. Go to your Vimeo video
2. Click **Privacy** → set to **Hide from Vimeo / Only people with a private link**
3. Copy the private video URL

Vimeo's privacy settings prevent direct search indexing. Buyers with the link can watch; everyone else gets a private video error.

### Google Drive (uploaded video file)

1. Upload your `.mp4` or `.mov` file to Google Drive
2. Right-click → **Share** → **Anyone with the link can view**
3. Copy the link

Buyers click the link and stream or download the file directly from Drive.

### YouTube (unlisted)

1. Set your YouTube video to **Unlisted** (not private, not public)
2. Copy the URL from the address bar

Unlisted YouTube videos are not searchable and do not appear in your channel. Only people with the URL can watch.
