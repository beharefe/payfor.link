Lemon Squeezy charges **5% + $0.50 per transaction**. The $0.50 is a flat fee that hits harder than the percentage on lower-priced products.

On a **$19 product**: $0.95 + $0.50 = $1.45 = **7.6% effective rate**

On a **$49 product**: $2.45 + $0.50 = $2.95 = **6% effective rate**

unseal.link charges **4.5% flat** with no per-transaction fee. The gap shrinks at high prices, but the math favors unseal.link on any product below ~$200.

## Lemon Squeezy

**Best for**: Developers selling software, plugins, or SaaS who need automatic international tax compliance as a Merchant of Record

**Fee**: 5% + $0.50 per transaction

**What it does well**:
- Merchant of Record: Lemon Squeezy handles EU VAT, GST, and sales tax globally. You never file or remit tax on international sales — they do it for you. This is genuinely valuable if you sell to customers across many jurisdictions.
- License key management: built-in license generation and validation, useful for software products
- Subscription and recurring billing support
- Webhook-based API for SaaS integrations

**Where it falls short**:
- The $0.50 flat fee is punishing on low-priced products
- Built around file uploads; no native URL-based delivery
- Overkill for creators selling Notion templates or Figma files
- Payout minimum of $50

**When Lemon Squeezy wins**: You are a developer selling a software product (plugin, app, tool) to an international audience including EU customers, and you want someone else to handle VAT compliance entirely. The higher fee is a reasonable price for that service.

## unseal.link

**Best for**: Creators selling link-based products (Notion, Figma, Google Drive, GitHub, Discord) who want the lowest fee with the fastest setup

**Fee**: 4.5% flat. No per-transaction fee. No monthly cost.

**What it does well**:
- No file upload required — paste any URL, set a price, share the paywall link
- Buyers pay via Stripe and unlock instantly after payment
- No buyer account needed
- Lowest effective fee on any product under $200
- Works with any URL: Notion, Figma, Google Drive, GitHub, Discord, Canva, Dropbox, Loom
- Payment before delivery — eliminates ghosting for freelancers

**Where it falls short**:
- No Merchant of Record: you are responsible for your own tax compliance on international sales
- No license key management
- No marketplace discovery: you bring your own audience
