## Protecting your Notion template

The most common question: "Can buyers share my link with others?"

Yes -- and that's true on Gumroad too. Once someone has a URL, they can share it. Your options:

1. **Accept it** -- most template buyers won't redistribute. They paid for it.
2. **Use Notion's duplicate-on-click** -- each buyer who duplicates gets their own copy, not your master template. They can't edit yours.
3. **Use a different Notion share link per tier** -- advanced, but possible for high-priced products.

For most template sellers, basic paywall protection is more than enough.

## What to price your Notion template

The floor is $9.99. Beyond that, it depends on:

- **Complexity**: A simple daily planner vs a full business OS are different price points
- **Your audience**: Twitter creators with 10k followers can charge more than day-one accounts
- **Uniqueness**: Does your template do something nobody else's does?
- **Perceived value**: Price to your buyer's outcome, not your hours

Common ranges:
- Simple templates (habit trackers, reading lists): $9.99-$19
- Business/productivity templates: $29-$49
- Full systems / dashboards: $49-$99
- Bundles: $79-$199

Start where you think buyers will say "obviously worth it" without hesitation.
