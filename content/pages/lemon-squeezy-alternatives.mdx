## Why people look for Lemon Squeezy alternatives

Lemon Squeezy charges **5% + $0.50 per transaction**. The percentage is fine. The $0.50 is the problem.

On a **$9 product**: $0.45 + $0.50 = $0.95 → **10.6% effective rate**
On a **$19 product**: $0.95 + $0.50 = $1.45 → **7.6% effective rate**
On a **$49 product**: $2.45 + $0.50 = $2.95 → **6.0% effective rate**

Below $50, the per-transaction fee pushes Lemon Squeezy's real rate well above what it looks like. If you sell lower-priced digital products, you are overpaying.

The other common reason: Lemon Squeezy is designed for software and SaaS. If you sell Notion templates, Figma files, or link-based products, you are using a tool built for a different use case.

## The alternatives compared

### 1. unseal.link — Best for link-based products, lowest effective fee

**Fee**: 4.5% flat. No per-transaction fee. No monthly cost.

**What it does**: Turn any URL into a paywall. Buyers pay via Stripe and receive the link by email. Works with Notion, Figma, Google Drive, Discord, GitHub, Dropbox, Loom — any URL.

**Best for**: Creators selling link-based digital products who want the lowest fee without a per-transaction charge or monthly commitment. Also ideal for freelancers who want payment before delivery.

**What it doesn't do**: No Merchant of Record (you handle your own taxes). No license key generation. No SaaS billing.

**Fee at $9**: 4.5% = $0.405 (vs Lemon Squeezy's $0.95)
**Fee at $49**: 4.5% = $2.205 (vs Lemon Squeezy's $2.95)

### 2. Gumroad — Best for marketplace discovery

**Fee**: 10% flat. No monthly cost.

**What it does**: Digital product marketplace with built-in buyer discovery. Storefront, affiliate tools, subscriber emails.

**Best for**: Creators with no existing audience who want the marketplace to find buyers. The 10% buys distribution.

**What it doesn't do**: No Merchant of Record. No license keys. Very high fee if you bring your own traffic.

### 3. Payhip — Best for EU VAT compliance at lower volume

**Fee**: 5% (free), 2% ($29/mo), 0% ($99/mo).

**What it does**: Full storefront, EU VAT handling as Merchant of Record, affiliate program, course hosting.

**Best for**: EU sellers who want tax compliance handled, and creators who need a full browsable storefront.

**What it doesn't do**: No URL-based delivery — file uploads required.

### 4. Paddle — Best for SaaS and software at scale

**Fee**: 5% + $0.50 (similar to Lemon Squeezy). Enterprise pricing available.

**What it does**: Merchant of Record for SaaS, enterprise billing, global tax compliance, subscription management.

**Best for**: Software companies selling internationally who need a full MoR infrastructure. Not designed for individual digital product creators.

### 5. Stripe directly — Best if you build your own checkout

**Fee**: 0% platform fee (Stripe processing fees apply: ~2.9% + $0.30).

**What it does**: Raw payment infrastructure. You build the checkout, product pages, fulfillment, and delivery.

**Best for**: Developers who want maximum control and minimum fees. Requires custom development work.

## When to stay on Lemon Squeezy

Lemon Squeezy built something genuinely useful for one specific use case: **developers selling software internationally who want automatic VAT compliance**. If you sell SaaS products, plugins, or tools to customers across the EU, US, and beyond, Lemon Squeezy's Merchant of Record service means you never file international taxes. That is worth the fee premium.

If your products are priced at $99+, the $0.50 per-transaction fee matters less (it represents 0.5% at $100). The platform makes more sense at higher price points.

Stay on Lemon Squeezy if: you sell software to international customers, need license key management, or want MoR tax compliance.

Switch if: you sell link-based products at typical creator prices ($9–$99), your effective rate is noticeably above 5%, or you are paying $0.50/transaction on a product that nets you $7.
