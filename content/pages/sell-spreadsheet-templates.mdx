## Spreadsheet templates are a real market

Buyers pay for spreadsheets that solve specific problems. The most commonly sold templates:

- **Personal finance**: Budget trackers, net worth calculators, debt payoff planners
- **Business finance**: P&L templates, cash flow models, startup financial models
- **Freelance/agency**: Client trackers, proposal calculators, invoice trackers
- **Project management**: Gantt charts, sprint trackers, roadmap templates
- **Marketing**: Content calendars, social media trackers, SEO dashboards
- **HR/operations**: Employee directories, onboarding checklists, OKR trackers
- **E-commerce**: Inventory trackers, profit margin calculators, COGS models

The market exists on Etsy, Gumroad, and a dozen other platforms — but those require file uploads and take 5–10% fees. Spreadsheet templates do not need file hosting. They are URLs.

## How to create a shareable link for each format

### Google Sheets

The cleanest option for spreadsheet templates:

1. Build your template in Google Sheets
2. Go to **File → Share → Share with others**
3. Change access to **"Anyone with the link"** and set to **Viewer**
4. Copy the link

**Important**: Share a "view only" copy link, not an edit link. Buyers will use **File → Make a copy** to get their own editable version. This is standard practice for Google Sheets templates.

Alternatively, publish a "make a copy" link by appending `/copy` to the base Sheets URL — this prompts buyers to immediately copy to their Drive.

### Excel / Microsoft Office

Two options:
- **OneDrive**: Upload your Excel file to OneDrive, click Share → set to "Anyone with the link can view" → copy the link. Buyers can download the file directly.
- **Google Drive**: Upload the `.xlsx` file to Google Drive, share with "Anyone with the link," copy the URL. Buyers download the Excel file.

### Airtable

Airtable offers a "Share a copy of this base" link. Buyers click it and get their own editable copy of your base directly in their Airtable account. No export required.
