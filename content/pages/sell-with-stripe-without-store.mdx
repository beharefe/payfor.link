## Stripe is powerful. Setting it up is not.

Stripe is what every serious business uses to process payments. It handles card networks, fraud detection, SCA compliance, and payouts to 40+ countries.

But to use Stripe directly, you need to:
- Build a checkout page (frontend code)
- Create a backend to initiate sessions and handle webhooks
- Set up Stripe Connect if you want to sell as yourself
- Handle email delivery of your product
- Test everything, fix edge cases, deploy

That is days or weeks of work. For most creators and freelancers, it is not worth doing from scratch.

unseal.link gives you the full Stripe Checkout flow for any URL-based product in 5 minutes. No code required.

## How payouts work (and why you don't need Stripe upfront)

You don't need a Stripe account to start. Create your products, share your paywall links, and when you're ready to receive payments, click "Enable payouts" in your dashboard.

At that point, unseal.link creates a Stripe Express account for you automatically — the same Stripe Connect OAuth flow used by Shopify, Airbnb, and Lyft. The whole setup takes about 2 minutes.

What this means:
- No prior Stripe account needed — we create it for you
- Payments from buyers go directly to your account
- unseal.link takes its 4.5% platform fee via Stripe's application fees API
- We never hold your money
- You can withdraw on your own schedule

Once payouts are enabled, all your products use it automatically.
