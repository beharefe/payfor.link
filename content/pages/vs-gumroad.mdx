## What Gumroad's 10% actually buys

Gumroad has genuine value for the right seller:

**Marketplace discovery**: Gumroad has millions of buyers browsing for digital products. If you have no existing audience, Gumroad's discovery engine can generate sales you would not otherwise get. For new creators, this is real value.

**Storefront and profiles**: A Gumroad profile is a browsable product catalog. Multiple products, follow buttons, subscriber emails. If people need to explore your catalog, Gumroad supports that.

**Built-in affiliates**: Gumroad has an affiliate system. If affiliate-driven sales are part of your model, it is built in.

When Gumroad wins: you are new, have no audience, and want the marketplace to find buyers for you. The 10% is the cost of that distribution.

## When unseal.link wins

You already have an audience — a newsletter, a Twitter following, a Discord, a community. You do not need Gumroad's marketplace because you are sending your own traffic. The 10% becomes a pure tax on sales you generated yourself.

You also win on link-based products. Gumroad requires file uploads. If your product is a Notion page, Figma file, Google Drive folder, or Discord invite, Gumroad forces a workaround. unseal.link treats any URL as a product natively.
