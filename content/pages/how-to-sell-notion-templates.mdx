## How Notion Template Links Work

When you share a Notion page with "Duplicate to my Notion" enabled, anyone with the link gets a full copy of your page (databases, views, properties, relations, and all) in their own Notion workspace. Your original is completely untouched.

That link is the product. Everything else is just delivery infrastructure.

---

## Getting Your Template Link

Open your Notion page, click **Share** in the top right, toggle "Share to web" ON, and copy the link. It'll look like `https://yourname.notion.site/Template-Name-abc123def456`.

One thing worth knowing: if your template uses connected databases that link to pages outside the template, those connections won't carry over to the buyer's copy. Test your template by duplicating it yourself before selling; catch any broken references before your buyers do.

## Where to Find Buyers

Twitter is the primary distribution channel for Notion creators. Post a screenshot of your template, specifically the most impressive view, the one that makes people think "I need that." Show the database structure, the filtered views, the linked relations working together. Text alone doesn't sell Notion templates; the visual does the work.

Reddit's r/Notion has a weekly self-promotion thread pinned at the top; use it. For business-focused templates, r/Entrepreneur and r/freelance are worth a post framed around the problem your template solves rather than the template itself.

The longer-term play: Notion template marketplaces like Prototion and Notion Everything have organic search traffic from people specifically looking to buy. List your template there (linking to your unseal.link for the purchase) and you get their SEO working for you.

## Pricing

The Notion template market has a wide range. Simple single-purpose templates (a reading tracker, a habit log) go $5-$15. Multi-database workspace systems that replace real software go $29-$79. Full business operating systems (CRM, project management, finance, team wiki in one) go $49-$149.

The creators making real money from Notion templates aren't competing on price, they're competing on niche specificity. A generic project manager template competes with hundreds of others. A Notion CRM specifically for freelance video editors has almost no competition and can charge more.

## FAQs

### Can buyers share my template link?

Yes, and it's the main downside of Notion's share system; the link isn't single-use. In reality, most buyers don't share it, and over-engineering protection costs more than it saves. The practical approach: add a brief license notice to your template's cover page, and periodically generate a new share link for high-volume products to invalidate old ones.

### Do buyers need a paid Notion plan?

They need a free Notion account at minimum. If your template uses features that require Notion Plus (certain database limits, advanced permissions), be upfront about that in your listing. Most templates work fine on the free plan.

### I already sell on Etsy. Should I switch?

Don't switch; add. Etsy gives you marketplace discovery; unseal.link gives you direct sales where you keep more. Run both simultaneously. Some sellers find that after a few months, direct sales via their own audience outperform Etsy even at lower traffic volume because the conversion rate is higher with warm audiences.

### How do I handle someone who wants a refund?

Full refund control is in your unseal.link dashboard. Since the template was delivered digitally, you're not legally required to refund in most jurisdictions, but for genuine misunderstandings, one discretionary refund costs less than a public complaint. It's your call each time.
