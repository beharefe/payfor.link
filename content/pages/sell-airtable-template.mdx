## How to create an Airtable share-a-copy link

Airtable has a built-in feature for template sharing:

1. Open the base you want to sell
2. Click the **Share** button in the top right
3. Scroll to **"Share a copy of the base"** (at the bottom of the share panel)
4. Click **"Generate shareable link"**
5. Copy the link — it looks like `https://airtable.com/shrXXXXXXXXXXXXX/copy`

When a buyer clicks this link, they are prompted to duplicate the base to their own Airtable account. They get a complete copy of your tables, views, fields, automations, and data structure. Your original base is unaffected.

## What to include in a good Airtable template

Higher-priced Airtable templates typically include:

- **Pre-populated example data** so buyers understand how to use the system
- **Multiple linked views** (grid, kanban, gallery, calendar) configured for common workflows
- **Automations pre-built** for repetitive tasks (status change notifications, etc.)
- **A companion Notion doc or Google Doc** explaining how to set it up (paste that link too — you can include it in the same paywall or as a separate purchase)
- **Field documentation** — comments or a legend sheet explaining each field's purpose
