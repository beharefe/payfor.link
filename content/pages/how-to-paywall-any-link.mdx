## The problem with how creators get paid today

You made something valuable. A Notion template. A Figma file. A Google Drive folder full of resources. A Discord community. A private GitHub repo.

You want to charge for it.

So you sign up for Gumroad. You spend two hours setting up a product page, a storefront, a bio, a cover image. You upload your file -- except your thing isn't a file, it's a Notion page or a Google Drive link, so you have to work around that.

Then you wait. Gumroad takes 10%.

Or you go the invoice route. You send an invoice. The client says "I'll pay it later." You deliver. They ghost. You spend a week chasing payment.

There's a better way.

## What a paywall link actually is

A paywall link is a URL that requires payment before revealing your content URL. You paste your destination link, set a price, and get a shareable page like `unseal.link/@yourname/your-product`.

Anyone who visits that page sees your product title and price. They pay via Stripe. They receive your original URL by email -- instantly.

Your content never moves. No uploads. No hosting. No platform lock-in.

## What you can paywall

Anything with a URL:

- **Notion templates** -- make the page public (view only), paste the link
- **Figma files** -- share with "anyone with the link can view", paste the link
- **Google Drive folders** -- set to "anyone with the link", paste the link
- **Discord servers** -- create an invite link, paste it
- **GitHub repos** -- works for public repos or you can add the buyer manually after
- **Loom videos** -- paste the private video link
- **Airtable bases** -- share with view-only link
- **Any downloadable file** -- host it anywhere with a direct link, paste it
- **Zoom / call booking links** -- paywall a Calendly link for paid consultations

## Why this beats Gumroad for link-based products

Gumroad is built around file uploads. If your product is a link, not a file, you're fighting the platform.

With unseal.link:
- No file upload needed -- paste a URL
- No storefront setup -- your paywall page is instant
- 4.5% fee vs Gumroad's 10%
- Buyers need no account -- just an email address

On a $49 template sold 50 times: you keep $127.75 more vs Gumroad. That's without changing your price or selling more.

## How to get paid before you deliver

The most common mistake freelancers and creators make: delivering work before payment.

A paywall link solves this structurally. There's no way to get the link without paying. You don't have to chase invoices. You don't have to say "please pay me first" awkwardly. The system enforces it.

Create a paywall for your:
- **Consultation notes / deliverables** -- create the doc, share it after payment
- **Freelance deliverables** -- put the final file link behind a paywall
- **Commission work** -- buyer pays for the final version, gets the download link
- **Course materials** -- paywall the course folder link
- **Templates and resources** -- sell once, deliver to everyone automatically
